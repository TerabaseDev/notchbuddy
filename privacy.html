The short version

Notch Buddy does not collect, sell, or share your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. Everything it shows is read on your Mac and stays on your Mac.

What Notch Buddy reads, and where it stays

FeatureWhat it readsWhere it is kept
ShelfReferences to files you drop on the notch. Files are not copied.On your Mac, in the app's settings
ClipboardText you copy, while the app runs. Items marked as passwords or concealed are skipped.Memory only. Gone when the app quits.
NotesThe note you typeA text file in the app's own folder on your Mac
Now PlayingThe track playing in Apple Music or Spotify, through AppleScriptMemory only
CalendarYour upcoming events, after you allow accessMemory only
DownloadsNames and dates of recent files in your Downloads folderMemory only
Screenshot and RecordingThe screen, window, or area you choose to captureImage and movie files in Pictures/Notch Buddy
ActivityCounts of shelf and clipboard use per dayOn your Mac, in the app's settings
Activity: AI usageToken counts from the local logs of Claude Code, Codex, and Grok. The Mac App Store version reads a tool's folder only after you choose it; the direct-download version reads ~/.claude, ~/.codex, and ~/.grok while the Activity tab is open. Only numbers are taken from the logs.Memory only
Battery, volume, brightness, CPU, memoryCurrent system readingsMemory only

macOS asks for your permission before Notch Buddy can use your calendars, record the screen, or control Music and Spotify. The direct-download version can also ask to read your Downloads folder and to control System Events (for the Light/Dark switch). You can turn any of these off in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Network connections

  • Spotify cover art. When Spotify reports a cover art address for the playing track, Notch Buddy downloads that image to display it. The request goes to the address Spotify provides and includes nothing about you beyond what any image download does.
  • Lyrics (direct-download version only). With Look Up Lyrics Online turned on in Settings, the song's title, artist, album, and length are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to find lyrics. The Mac App Store version never does this.

Notch Buddy makes no other network connections.

Children

Notch Buddy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
