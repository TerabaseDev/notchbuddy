Getting started

Notch Buddy needs macOS 15 or later. It lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon.

  • Open it: hover over the notch, or click it. On a Mac without a notch, use the small pill at the top center of the screen.
  • Switch tabs: use the icons on the left of the open notch.
  • Settings: click the Notch Buddy icon in the menu bar, then Settings. Turn modules on or off, reorder tabs, and choose a size and accent color.
  • Keep it open: use the pin button in the notch, or Pin Notch Open in the menu bar.

Permissions

macOS asks before Notch Buddy can use some features. If you said no, you can change it in System Settings > Privacy & Security:

  • Calendars for the Calendar tab.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording for Screenshot and Recording. After you turn it on, quit and reopen Notch Buddy; macOS applies it only after a relaunch.
  • Automation for Now Playing, so Notch Buddy can read and control Apple Music or Spotify.

Common questions

Where are my screenshots and recordings?

In the Notch Buddy folder inside your Pictures folder. You can also drag the thumbnail out of the notch into any app.

The Activity tab asks me to choose a folder.

The Mac App Store version can only read the usage logs of Claude Code, Codex, or Grok after you pick that tool's folder (for example ~/.claude). Only token counts are read, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Can it turn on Do Not Disturb?

No. macOS doesn't let apps change Focus, so Notch Buddy doesn't offer it.

Does it collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar icon, then move Notch Buddy from Applications to the Trash.
